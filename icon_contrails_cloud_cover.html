Humidity. ICON reports relative humidity over water (RHw). Vapour pressure e = RHw · esat(w)(T), and RH over ice RHi = e / esat(i)(T). Saturation vapour pressures use the Tetens formula esat(T) = a1 exp[a3(T − T0)/(T − a4)], T0 = 273.16 K, with a1 = 611.21 Pa, a3 = 17.502, a4 = 32.19 K over water (Buck 1981) and a1 = 611.21 Pa, a3 = 22.587, a4 = −0.7 K over ice (Alduchov & Eskridge 1996).

Cloud cover. The cross-section shows cloud cover parameterised after Sundqvist et al. (1989): CC = 1 − √((1 − RHphase)/(1 − RHcrit)), with RHcrit = 80 % (CC = 0 for RHphase ≤ 80 %, CC = 100 % for RHphase ≥ 100 %). RHphase = e / esat, where esat follows the cloud phase: esat(i) for T ≤ 250.16 K, esat(w) for T ≥ 273.16 K, and α esat(w) + (1 − α) esat(i) in between, with α = ((T − 250.16 K)/23 K)². The mid and low cloud bars show the model's own mid and low cloud cover.

Contrails. Tc = critical temperature for contrail formation from Schrader (1997), Tables A1–A4, looked up with RHw. The tables are interpolated linearly in ln p, in √(1 − RHw) and in ln CF; below 500 hPa Tc is extrapolated in ln p. Contrails form where T < Tc and are persistent where, in addition, RHi reaches the chosen threshold. The contrail strip summarises each hour over 150–500 hPa (the table range).

Display. Levels below the model surface (pressure higher than the surface pressure) are masked. Right axis: approximate flight level in the ICAO standard atmosphere.
